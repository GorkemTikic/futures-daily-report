02 · Positioning

How traders were leaning

Funding shows which side is paying to hold its position (positive = longs pay shorts). Open interest is how much money is in open bets; its change over the day shows money coming in or out. Long/short is how many accounts lean each way. Top L/S is the ratio among the largest traders. Taker B/S is the buy-to-sell ratio of market orders.
