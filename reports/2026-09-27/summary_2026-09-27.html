07 · Glossary

Every term used today, in plain words

Close — The last price recorded at the end of the trading day (23:59 UTC for this report).
Exchange (venue) — A marketplace where traders buy and sell crypto—in this report, Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget, and Gate.
Funding rate — A periodic payment between long and short traders on a perpetual contract; not available for this date.
Long/short ratio — The balance between traders betting the price will go up (long) and traders betting it will go down (short); not available for this date.
Mark price — The reference price used to calculate gains and losses on perpetual contracts; not available for this date.
Open interest — The total amount of money traders have locked into perpetual contracts; not available for this date.
Perpetual contract (perp) — A futures contract with no expiration date that lets traders bet on price with leverage.
Volume — The total dollar value of trades executed during the day; shows which exchanges had the most trading activity.
