07 · Glossary

Every term used today, in plain words

Basis — The time period used to calculate price change and volume, typically UTC calendar day
Breadth — The percentage of Binance perpetual contracts trading higher on the day; a measure of broad market momentum
ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund) — An investment fund that trades on an exchange like stocks; spot Bitcoin and Ethereum ETFs hold actual coins and track their price
ETF flows — Net inflows (deposits) or outflows (withdrawals) into exchange-traded funds; negative flows mean investors are pulling money out
Exchange delisting — When a crypto platform stops offering trading for a particular token
Fear-Greed Index — A sentiment gauge from 0 (Extreme Fear) to 100 (Extreme Greed) measuring investor emotion; Extreme Fear means markets are panicked
Funding rate — A periodic payment between traders holding long and short positions on perpetual futures, designed to keep prices close to spot price; positive rates mean longs pay shorts
Funding rate annualized — The funding rate projected over a full year; a 9% annualized rate means longs are paying shorts about 9% per year
IP address — A unique number that identifies a computer or device on a network; used for routing internet traffic
Liquidity — The ease with which an asset can be bought or sold; high liquidity means you can trade large amounts without moving the price
Liquidity providers — Users who deposit funds into decentralized exchanges or lending pools to earn fees; the first to suffer losses in exploits
Long/Short ratio — The ratio of traders betting prices will go up (longs) to those betting prices will go down (shorts); above 1 means more bullish positioning
Mark price — The reference price used in perpetual futures contracts; prevents price manipulation by smoothing spot price
Open interest — The total value of all open leveraged positions on a contract; rising OI can indicate new traders entering, falling OI means positions are closing
Redemption — When an investor sells their shares of an ETF back to the fund manager for cash
Spot price — The current market price for immediate delivery of a coin, as opposed to futures contracts which settle later
Taker buy/sell ratio — The ratio of market buy orders (takers) to sell orders; above 1 means more aggressive buying pressure
Tokenized — Converted into a digital token on a blockchain; e.g., tokenized stocks are stock ownership records on blockchain instead of traditional certificates
Top trader long/short ratio — The ratio of long to short positions held by the largest traders on an exchange; indicates what institutional-sized traders believe will happen
Venue — A crypto exchange or trading platform where futures contracts are traded
Volume — The total value of all trades on a contract during a time period; high volume suggests active trading
