07 · Glossary

Every term used today, in plain words

Annualized percentage (for funding rates) — A funding rate stated as if it continued for a full year. An 8-hour funding rate of 0.00006 annualizes to 6.66% because there are 1,095 such intervals in a year (365 days × 3 per day).
Breadth — The percentage of all trading pairs (cryptocurrencies) that went up in price during the day. Low breadth (like 9%) means most coins fell; high breadth means most rose. Shows whether a move is broad-based or driven by a few coins.
Fear-and-greed index — A number from 0 to 100 measuring trader emotion. 0–25 is extreme fear, 25–45 is fear, 45–55 is neutral, 55–75 is greed, 75–100 is extreme greed. Higher numbers mean traders expect prices to rise; lower numbers mean they expect them to fall.
Funding rate — A payment between traders betting prices go up (longs) and traders betting prices go down (shorts). Positive rates mean longs pay shorts; negative rates mean shorts pay longs. Reflects whether traders expect prices to rise or fall.
Long / long position — A bet that a cryptocurrency's price will go up. If a trader is 'long' Bitcoin, they own it or are using leverage to amplify an ownership position.
Long/short ratio — The number of traders betting on price increases (longs) divided by the number betting on price decreases (shorts). A ratio above 1.0 means more longs; below 1.0 means more shorts.
Mark price — The reference price used to calculate funding payments and margin requirements on a leveraged trading exchange. Usually close to the real-time market price but may differ slightly.
Open interest — The total amount of cryptocurrency (in coins or USD value) that traders have borrowed to take leveraged positions. High open interest means many leveraged bets are outstanding; low open interest means fewer. When open interest falls, traders are closing positions.
Open-interest change — How much the total open interest grew or shrank during the day. A decline usually means traders reduced leverage, either because they closed winning bets or cut losses.
Short / short position — A bet that a cryptocurrency's price will go down. A trader using leverage to short Bitcoin expects the price to fall and profits if it does.
Taker buy/sell ratio — A measure of aggressive trading direction. Takers are traders buying or selling immediately at market price. A ratio of 0.863 means for every $0.863 of aggressive buying, there was $1.00 of aggressive selling. Below 1.0 indicates net selling pressure.
Top trader position ratio — Among the largest traders on an exchange, the ratio of those betting on price increases (longs) to those betting on price decreases (shorts). Shows which direction the biggest players are positioned. A ratio of 1.87 means the largest traders are heavily betting on price increases.
