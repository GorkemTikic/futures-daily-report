07 · Glossary

Every term used today, in plain words

Annualized percentage — A rate expressed as if it were applied for a full year. If a funding rate is 0.0000509 per 8 hours and annualized, it equals roughly 5.6% per year.
Basis — The time period used to calculate price changes and volume. 'UTC-day' means 00:00–23:59 UTC on a given date; 'rolling-24h' means the most recent 24 hours from the reporting time.
Funding rate — A periodic fee exchanged between traders on derivatives exchanges. When funding is positive, traders betting on higher prices pay traders betting on lower prices to hold their positions. Higher positive funding suggests bullish sentiment (more demand to go long). Funding rates reset every 8 hours on most major exchanges.
Long position — A bet that an asset's price will rise. A trader holding a long Bitcoin position profits if Bitcoin's price increases.
Long/short ratio — The proportion of traders betting prices will go up versus down at a given time. A ratio of 1.5 means 1.5 traders are long for every 1 trader short. Higher ratios indicate more bullish sentiment.
Mark price — The reference price used by an exchange to calculate trader profits and losses on futures contracts. It is typically a blend of recent trades, an index price, and the underlying spot price, designed to prevent manipulation.
Multisig wallet — A cryptocurrency wallet controlled by multiple private keys, typically held by different parties. Transactions require approval from a threshold number of those keys (e.g., 3 of 5), making theft more difficult.
Open interest — The total dollar value (or coin quantity) of all open long and short contracts on a futures exchange. Rising open interest suggests traders are adding new positions; falling open interest suggests traders are closing existing positions.
Open-interest change — The percentage increase or decrease in total open positions over a reporting period. A +1% change means traders added new bets equal to 1% of the previous total open interest.
Short position — A bet that an asset's price will fall. A trader holding a short Bitcoin position profits if Bitcoin's price decreases.
Volume — The total dollar value of trades (buys and sells combined) executed during a period. Higher volume typically indicates more active trading and liquidity.
