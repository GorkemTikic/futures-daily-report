07 · Glossary

Every term used today, in plain words

Basis point (bp) — One-hundredth of a percent (0.01%). A 25 basis point rate hike means raising the rate by 0.25%.
Bridge (crypto) — A service that transfers crypto assets between two separate blockchains by locking them on one chain and releasing equivalent tokens on another.
Cloture vote — A procedural vote in the US Senate to end debate and force a final vote on a bill. Requires 60 votes to pass.
Federal Funds Rate — The interest rate at which commercial banks lend money to each other overnight. Set as a target range by the Federal Reserve.
FOMC (Federal Open Market Committee) — The policy-making committee of the US Federal Reserve that meets eight times per year to set monetary policy.
Funding rate — A periodic payment between traders holding long and short positions on perpetual futures contracts. Positive funding means long traders pay short traders.
HBM (High-Bandwidth Memory) — A type of computer memory chip used in advanced AI and graphics processors. High demand from AI chipmakers has benefited memory-chip manufacturers like Samsung and SK Hynix.
Leverage — Borrowing money to amplify the size of a trade. Higher leverage increases both potential gains and potential losses.
Open interest (OI) — The total dollar value of all outstanding derivative contracts (futures, perpetuals, options) on an asset. High OI indicates significant leverage in the market.
Perpetual futures (perps) — Derivative contracts that allow traders to bet on price movements without an expiry date, like spot trading but with leverage.
Stablecoins — Cryptocurrencies designed to maintain a stable price, typically pegged to the US dollar (e.g., USDC, USDT).
Synthetic (syBTC, etc.) — A token issued on one blockchain that represents an asset from another blockchain (e.g., syBTC represents Bitcoin but lives on another chain). If the underlying collateral is lost or minted without backing, the synthetic token loses value.
Venue — An exchange or trading platform where crypto futures are bought and sold.
