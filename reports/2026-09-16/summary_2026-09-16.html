07 · Glossary

Every term used today, in plain words

Basis point — One hundredth of a percent (0.01%). Interest rate changes are often described in basis points—for example, a 25 basis point rate hike means a 0.25% increase.
Funding rate — A fee paid between traders on perpetual futures exchanges. When funding is positive, traders betting on price rises pay traders betting on price falls. A higher funding rate means traders are very confident about higher prices.
Long position — A bet that an asset's price will go up.
Long/short ratio — The number of traders betting on price increases divided by the number betting on decreases. A ratio above 1.0 means more traders bet on up than down.
Mark price — The official reference price an exchange uses for a cryptocurrency at any moment, used to calculate profits and losses on futures contracts.
Open interest — The total number of active buy and sell contracts (longs and shorts combined) on a futures exchange at any moment.
Open interest change — The percentage increase or decrease in total active contracts on a futures exchange during a time period, showing whether traders are opening new bets or closing old ones.
Perpetual futures — Cryptocurrency contracts that never expire, allowing traders to bet on price moves using borrowed money (leverage) without a settlement date.
Short position — A bet that an asset's price will go down.
